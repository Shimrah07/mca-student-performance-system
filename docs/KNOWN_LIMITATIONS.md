# Known System Limitations & Future Research Directions

**Project Title:** AI-Based Student Performance Prediction & Academic Support System  
**Academic Program:** Master of Computer Applications, UIT-RGPV (Autonomous), Bhopal  
**Phase:** Final Release Readiness  

---

## 1. Machine Learning & Training Data Limitations

### 1.1 Synthetic Dataset Dependency
- **Context**: The classification models (Logistic Regression and Random Forest) were trained exclusively on a calibrated synthetic MCA dataset (6,370 subject rows and 1,200 semester rows).
- **Limitation**: Synthetic distributions, although mathematically calibrated to reflect UIT-RGPV autonomous grading statistics, cannot fully capture behavioral nuances, non-linear study patterns, socio-economic factors, or health emergencies that influence real student performance.
- **Academic Ethics**: Predictions generated by this system must strictly be treated as prototype demonstration outputs and early decision-support alerts. They must **never** be used as authoritative grading determinants, disciplinary justification, or grounds for denying student advancement.

### 1.2 Uncalibrated Probability Estimates
- **Context**: The model outputs class probabilities using `predict_proba()`.
- **Limitation**: While class probabilities sum to 1.0 and provide relative confidence rankings across the four tiers, they represent raw algorithmic decision scores and have not undergone post-hoc isotonic or Platt calibration on real student longitudinal cohorts.
- **Recommendation**: Model confidence percentages should be interpreted as diagnostic indicators rather than exact statistical likelihoods.

## 2. Curriculum & Syllabus Metadata Limitations

### 2.1 Provisional Semester 2 & Semester 4 Metadata
- **Context**: The UIT-RGPV Autonomous MCA curriculum operates under July 2025 (Semester 1) and July 2024 (Semester 3) official published schemes.
- **Limitation**: Official subject codes and credit breakdowns for Semester 2 and Semester 4 have not yet been formally published by the university autonomous board.
- **Safety Compliance**: In strict adherence to academic honesty, course names and subjects for Semesters 2 and 4 are confirmed from syllabus records, but their course codes remain cataloged as `"—"` and credits as `"TBC"`. No artificial course numbers or credits have been fabricated.

---

## 3. Platform & Operational Limitations

### 3.1 Single Academic Institution Focus
- The system rules, credit structures, 4-tier grade mappings, and semester timelines are designed specifically for the autonomous MCA program at UIT-RGPV Bhopal.
- Deployment to other universities or undergraduate disciplines would require reconfiguring scheme rules, formative assessment components (e.g. replacing MSTs with internal sessional tests), and retuning the model pipelines.

### 3.2 Single-Session Localhost Development Focus
- The current implementation is designed as a standalone academic exhibition prototype where the FastAPI server and Vite dev server run locally or as a lightweight cloud deployment.
- High-concurrency load testing, distributed Celery worker task queues, and multi-tenant department partitioning were outside the approved minor project scope and remain prime candidates for major project expansion.
